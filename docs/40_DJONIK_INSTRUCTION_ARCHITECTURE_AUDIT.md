# Djonik — аудит архітектури інструкцій і валідації #38

Дата: 2026-09-23. Статус: **пропозиція, лише аудит джерел**. Канонічний NOW — [#38](https://github.com/danielkokr/djonik-manager-2.0/issues/38), а не новий implementation issue. Жодних змін Agent, Skills, runtime чи roadmap цей документ не робить.

## 1. Executive conclusion

Поточна система **не має одного доступного coordinator контракту для спільних фактичних значень Trello**. Core задає fresh Trello > Memory, а `task-management` задає direct read > search. Проте `lastActivityAt`, completion timestamp, Waiting/Blocked, доказ прогресу та обмеження висновків із due/capacity деталізовані в `project-health` і specialist prompt, яких production coordinator **не має** як власний Skill. Daily/weekly planning використовують ті самі поля й статуси за слабшими правилами. Саме clean Sonnet 5 medium B і D продемонстрували цей розрив [39 §Clean](39_ISSUE_38_SONNET_5_LOW_MODEL_MIGRATION_REPORT.md#clean-medium-revalidation--independent-sessions-2026-09-23).

Це не виправдовує фактично хибні відповіді: «виконали 15-го», «сьогодні вже торкався», «Waiting — блоковане» та «запас є» не випливають із прочитаних даних. Але **якість відповіді, наявність явного active instruction і придатність тесту вимірювати model compliance — різні оцінки**. Повторні граматичні помилки, процесовий наратив і хибний weekday за явними правилами лишаються справжніми model failures. Project Health exact echo — передусім невдала композиція двох генеративних ланок при вже наявному deterministic `specialist_only` шляху.

Рекомендація: короткий глобальний семантичний блок у coordinator system prompt, вузькі domain Skills, незмінені deterministic boundaries для зовнішніх side effects і фактичних блоків; спеціаліст зберігає власний незалежний contract. Перебудувати #38 rubric із цього контракту і переоцінити наявні записи офлайн **до** будь-якого нового модельного експерименту. Наявні докази **не обґрунтовують** Sonnet 5 medium → high/stronger model: є невирівняні інструкції, помилки критеріїв і composition problem. Це не є твердженням, що medium уже прийнятий.

## 3. Root cause: три незалежні шари

1. **Instruction architecture (основна системна вада B/D).** Core [djonik:99–103](../managed-agents/djonik.md) каже «fresh reads» і «fact vs judgement», але не визначає, *що саме* доводить `lastActivityAt`, `dueComplete`, Waiting чи due. [task-management:93–97](../.claude/skills/task-management/SKILL.md) забороняє field facts із search, однак [task-management:65–67](../.claude/skills/task-management/SKILL.md) говорить про створення дедлайну, не про висновки щодо capacity. [daily-planning:16–31](../.claude/skills/daily-planning/SKILL.md) ранжує due/list/«blockers»/capacity без доказового словника; [weekly-planning:16–34](../.claude/skills/weekly-planning/SKILL.md) навіть називає картки з due цього тижня «hard deadlines». [project-health:36–64](../.claude/skills/project-health/SKILL.md) має потрібні обмеження, але це contract іншого Agent.
2. **Validation methodology.** Відсутність семантичного правила не робить unsupported факт істинним, але не дає класифікувати його як порушення *явної active instruction*. Окремі voice FAIL наклали жорсткішу форму, ніж [djonik:89–95](../managed-agents/djonik.md) і issue #38: system допускає 6–8 коротких Telegram рядків за замовчуванням і факти, що потрібні для відповіді; «Коротко» посилює стислiсть, але не задає ≤3 рядки/≤3 bullets. Приклад B у першому medium Session був забруднений попереднім A; [39:291–305](39_ISSUE_38_SONNET_5_LOW_MODEL_MIGRATION_REPORT.md) це виправляє. Після clean B сам факт згадки Extract уже підтверджений, не FAIL.
3. **Реальна model behavior.** Чіткі правила **були** доступні для природної української, відсутності процесового наративу, одного питання в одному реченні та верифікованих дат/weekday. `docs/36/37` фіксують їх порушення на Haiku; `docs/39` фіксує у medium процесовий наратив B, деякі мовні дефекти C, надмірну впевненість у ненауковій числовій «1–2 години» C2. Водночас C2 — не Trello hallucination; це непідтверджений загальний емпіричний claim. Low D wrong weekday — явне порушення core [djonik:101](../managed-agents/djonik.md), хоч це low, не medium.

## 5. Cross-Skill gaps and conflicts

1. **B completion-date:** direct card data `Done`, `dueComplete:true`, `lastActivityAt:15 Sep` confirms state and activity, never the Done transition timestamp. `task-management` requires direct read but lacks a completion-time meaning rule. The detached `work-review` Skill cannot save this path. [39:320–326,379–381](39_ISSUE_38_SONNET_5_LOW_MODEL_MIGRATION_REPORT.md); [task:93–97](../.claude/skills/task-management/SKILL.md).
2. **D activity-as-work:** `daily-planning` may rank `In progress`; it never defines `lastActivityAt`. PH explicitly says it is only Trello activity, not Daniel's work or staleness. «сьогодні вже торкався» crosses that missing contract. [39:363–385](39_ISSUE_38_SONNET_5_LOW_MODEL_MIGRATION_REPORT.md); [PH:58–64](../.claude/skills/project-health/SKILL.md).
3. **Waiting/Blocked:** `weekly-planning` collapses both in one bucket (line 22), daily uses “explicit blockers (waiting on someone/something)” (line 22) and even lists `blocked` as reason to leave out work (line 31). PH says waiting may need monitoring and blocked needs evidence of a preventing dependency. Clean D rendered all Waiting as «блоковане». **Semantic contradiction/ambiguity**, not merely missing copy.
4. **Due meanings:** weekly line 20 labels every Trello due this week a “hard deadline”, while product [00:155–166](00_DJONIK_PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md) distinguishes client commitments from internal due dates, PH line 42 and specialist line 56 reject the implication. Due should influence review/planning, but a verified timestamp alone does not prove external commitment, sufficiency, risk or spare capacity. Daily line 20 “how soon” is legitimate ordering *if* current date/zone is reliable; “запас є” requires workload/availability evidence absent in D.
5. **Date instructions genuinely differ by operation:** PH forbids *any model-derived* relative time and generally quotes ISO (PH 52–56); task write path asks the model to convert/compute and self-check weekday (task 111–131), while runtime has accepted deterministic due rendering for verified mutations (`src/djonikClient.ts` due finalization). The PH restriction is domain-specific; the **common** rule should be “never assert a derived weekday/local/relative time without a trusted formatter or explicit authoritative evidence.” Preserve PH's tighter policy. Retire obsolete model arithmetic from task wording only in a separate bounded source change, after checking which read-only path still needs dates.
6. **Board/project discovery:** PH Skill says find board first then label; task Skill says Daniel's work lives on one board, project is label. Specialist can still discover conservatively, but the two texts encode different environment assumptions. Do not make label IDs global constants; align a single source of current board/project identity and scope, retaining fresh board-label resolution for writes.
7. **Duplication:** fresh-read/Memory in core + daily + weekly + PH + specialist; verification in core + task + intake + daily/weekly handoff; clarification in core + task + each Skill; voice in core + each Skill. Repetition is useful only where scope differs (e.g. intake source order, PH read-only). The rest raises drift risk and token cost.
8. **Historical vs current:** `work-review` is unattached and #29 current-state-only guidance superseded by #36 action-history renderer. `docs/30` describes the pre-#40 bug; `docs/01` §13 and current `src/customToolResolution.ts` own the accepted continuation rule. #31/#32 accepted deterministic outcomes supersede any idea that a prompt alone can verify mutations, complete partial turns or safely relay unverifiable specialist content. #18 fresh-read-on-every-relevant-turn remains a model instruction/platform limitation, **not** a runtime guarantee ([PH:22](../.claude/skills/project-health/SKILL.md)).

## 7. Proposed global factual semantics — minimal coordinator wording

One compact core block (wording proposal, **not applied**):

> For current Trello facts, read fresh data; search discovers candidates, while direct card/list/board reads establish fields. Memory is durable context, never current task state. State what the evidence shows separately from your PM judgement. `lastActivityAt` means only Trello activity: it proves neither Daniel worked, progress, completion time nor staleness. `Done`/`dueComplete` indicate current completion state, not when completion happened; only an action-history event can date a transition. `Waiting` means waiting; call work `Blocked` only when a concrete dependency prevents progress. A card `due` is a recorded date, not proof of a client commitment, available capacity, low urgency or sufficient time. Do not derive local/relative date wording or actor/work history from raw metadata without authoritative evidence. Ask one question only when a missing identity or fact materially changes the response/action.

This is about **semantic entailment**, not a list of every forbidden sentence. It must be present for all coordinator turns where no Skill is read. Shorter version is acceptable if all six required propositions survive. Domain Skills may add stricter rules but must not weaken it. The specialist must receive an equivalent **independent** baseline through its own prompt/Skill because Managed Agents do not automatically share coordinator instructions ([01:189–195](01_CLAUDE_NATIVE_ARCHITECTURE.md)).

### Where to put it

| Option | Guaranteed visible to coordinator? | Token/duplication/maintenance | Managed Agents fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Concise core section | Yes when deployed in the resolved Agent/Session; no dependence on Skill activation | Small always-on overhead; one owner; source↔Agent sync required | **Best for cross-domain invariant**. Existing `managed-agents/djonik.md` already owns source. |
| B. Shared foundational Skill | **No guarantee** it is read on each relevant turn; attaching it is not the same as proving model consulted it. Requires explicit version/attachment | Lower per-turn content only if selectively read, but drift if domains echo it; another Skill and activation contract | Poor as sole safety boundary; #18 illustrates limits of conditional read. |
| C. Core + shared Skill | Core still required; Skill can explain edge cases/examples | More tokens and two sources for same meaning unless Skill is genuinely reference-only | Unnecessary now; reconsider only if numerous measured edge cases exceed concise core. |
| D. Memory/Advisor/custom router | Memory is mutable context; Advisor/new router adds uncertain delegation or code | Higher complexity and source ambiguity | No measured need, conflicts with small Claude-native architecture. |

**Choose A.** Keep core wording short; specialist's separate prompt/Skill holds its own domain-specific stricter copy only because it is a separate Agent, not to paper over coordinator activation. Source sync and version attestation are part of later bounded implementation/validation, not this audit.

## 9. Project Health composition recommendation

Asking an LLM to reproduce a generated specialist string **byte-for-byte** is a brittle transport protocol. [36:19–20](36_ISSUE_38_LIVE_VOICE_VALIDATION.md), [37:19](37_ISSUE_38_TARGETED_LIVE_REVALIDATION.md), [39:202–227](39_ISSUE_38_SONNET_5_LOW_MODEL_MIGRATION_REPORT.md) show a correct specialist result received unchanged, then paraphrased by the coordinator, then a visible fixed notice. The runtime protected factual authority correctly; the demanded coordinator behavior is poorly matched to generation. `specialist_only` already exists and returns exact specialist bytes when coordinator final text is empty ([turnCorrelation:314–323](../src/turnCorrelation.ts)). **Recommend** treating it as preferred read-only PH completion protocol, subject to a separately scoped design/test; do not change composition in #38 audit.

For mixed intent (e.g. «стан Extract і план на сьогодні»), the current event stream cannot prove whether arbitrary coordinator prose is independent planning or a rewrite. Preserve #32 guarantees: child identity/version and current-turn engagement, exactly one valid child result, authoritative `end_turn`, exact specialist bytes, fail-visible ambiguous/missing/duplicate outcomes, safe write finalization, FIFO and no late-child leak. Do not silently drop the ordinary request or pretend PH alone satisfied it. A bounded future design can prefer **sequential user-visible segments** only if provenance can be shown without parsing prose; otherwise explicitly tell Daniel that the second requested part needs a separate turn (current notice behavior), or use a separately justified structured boundary. Do not add an intent router or silently concatenate unverified coordinator text. Check whether a Managed Agents native mechanism yields provenance before modifying runtime.
